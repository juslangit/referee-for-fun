# Where the sport pictograms came from

The locked sports on the selection screen use Olympic-style pictograms from Wikimedia
Commons. They are in the **public domain**, so no credit is legally required — it is
here because everything else downloaded into this project carries one, and a reader
should not have to wonder which files are safe.

The four finished sports are not pictograms. They are `card_badminton.png`,
`card_beachvolleyball.png`, `card_volleyball.png` and `card_tennis.png` — photographs
of this game rendered by `dev/_cards.gd`, and they are ours. The split is deliberate:
a real picture on the selection screen means a sport you can actually walk into.

`sport_tennis.png`, `sport_volleyball.png` and `sport_beachvolleyball.png` are kept
because those sports were locked once and may need a placeholder again.

- **sport_tabletennis.png** — from Table tennis pictogram.svg by Thadius856 (SVG conversion) &amp; Parutakupiu (original image) (Public domain)
