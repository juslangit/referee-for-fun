The ideaA role nobody plays

Every sports game hands you the racket. This one hands you the chair.

  • You never hit the ballYou sit in the umpire's chair and watch.
  • You decide what is trueThe point goes wherever you say it went.
  • Nothing stops you lyingExcept everyone in the building watching you do it.

How it playsOne point, three beats

01

The rally is real

Full physics. The game knows exactly where it landed — to the millimetre.

02

You make the call

Left click IN, right click OUT. Plus lets and faults.

03

The room reacts

Crowd, players, coaches. The game never tells you if you got away with it.

The game knows the truth.
It is never going to tell you.

The temptationWhy you'd lie

Somebody wants a result.

  • 01The event needs a certain name in the final.
  • 02The panel marks whether anything happened.
  • 03A grudge from last season, kept in your save.
  • 04Your own mistake, begging to be evened out.

No bribes. Nobody ever asks.

The pushbackHow you get caught

Everyone is watching the chair.

  • 01Line judges call it too. Overrule them and it shows.
  • 02Hawk-Eye replays the truth in front of everyone.
  • 03Reputation, one number out of 100, follows you everywhere.

Being wrong is fine. Being wrong for the same side every time is not.
