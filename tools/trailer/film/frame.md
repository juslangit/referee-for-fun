- Accent `#FFC22E` (the HUD caption yellow), ink on accent `#1A1400` · team red `#D63C36` · team blue `#2E6FD0`
- Display: Barlow Condensed Bold 700, uppercase for titles, from `assets/fonts/BarlowCondensed-Bold.ttf`
- Recurring device: the broadcast bar — dark pill, 10 px yellow left rule, yellow tab with dark ink.
- Motion: hard sports-broadcast moves — wipes from the left, slams with a short overshoot, no floaty fades.
