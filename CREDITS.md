The four athletes, the spectator, their rig and all twelve animations were built for
this game and are not from anywhere. They are generated by
`tools/blender/character_forge.py`, which can be re-run at any time:

    blender --background --python tools/blender/character_forge.py

The sepak takraw animations (the `st_` clips) were keyed for this game by hand, as numbers,
in `tools/meshy/takraw_clips.py`.

The crowd behind the front row, the hall, the court, the net and the umpire chair are
built in code.

## Interface art
The title logo and the first five athlete portraits on the sport tiles were generated for
this game with OpenArt (Nano Banana 2) on 2026-09-15, from concept mockups made the same way.
The originals are in `dev/ref/ui-redesign/`; `tools/ui/prepare_art.gd` cuts the portraits
apart and keys the logo's background out. The sixth, sepak takraw's, was generated
separately with Nano Banana 2 later the same day, using that sheet of five as its style
reference. Its original is in `dev/ref/takraw/`, and the same script fits it to the sheet's
framing.

## Event art
The six event logos, the twelve sponsors and the supporters' banners round the venues were
generated for this game with OpenArt (GPT Image 2) on 2026-09-15 — the first five logos and the
rest in the morning, sepak takraw's logo on its own later that day. Every event and sponsor
name is invented. The originals are in `dev/ref/events/`; `tools/events/prepare_event_art.gd`
keys, cuts and trims them into `assets/events/`.
